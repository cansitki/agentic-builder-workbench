# To Do List

> The single general task list. Project backlogs hold detail and link back here.

## Rules

- New or unclear work goes to Inbox.
- In Progress has a limit of 2; the first item is the main quest.
- Next has a limit of 7 and is ordered deliberately.
- Move one item between sections; do not copy it.
- Record a blocker, dependency, and review trigger.
- Complete only after verification and include the date/evidence.
