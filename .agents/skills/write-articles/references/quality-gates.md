## Critical failures

Any of these blocks approval:

- invented statistic, quotation, customer, result, source, URL, or first-hand experience;
- a citation that does not support the exact claim;
- several secondary pages presented as independent evidence when they repeat the same unsupported origin;
- hidden uncertainty or a causal claim presented as fact;
- new facts added during localization;
- client work used without the required naming or attribution permission;
- current legal, market, price, platform, or economic claims left unverified;
- the article exists mainly to manipulate search rankings;
- publication or deployment without explicit authorization.

## Hard caps

Treat the draft as a major rewrite when:

- it is only the seed expanded with filler;
- the thesis or reader need remains unclear;
- a material contradictory source is ignored;
- the structure is a landing page with repeated promotion;
- claims and evidence cannot be separated;
- most headings could sit under any agency’s logo unchanged.

## Weighted review rubric

Score each dimension from `0` to `4`, then apply the weight.

| Dimension | Weight | What a 4 requires |
|---|---:|---|
| Requirements and format | 10 | Fulfils the requested mode, audience, state, language, and constraints |
| Thesis and editorial angle | 15 | Clear, distinct, useful, and more valuable than the seed |
| Claims and source integrity | 20 | Traceable claims, correct scope, counterevidence and limits preserved |
| Structure and reasoning | 15 | Reader-led progression, mechanisms, alternatives, informative headings |
| Voice and sentence quality | 15 | Specific, natural, consistent, varied, and brand-appropriate |
| Reader usefulness | 10 | Provides a practical diagnostic, method, example, or decision |
| Brand fit and restraint | 10 | Perspective is recognizable without turning into a sales page |
| Editorial package | 5 | Accurate metadata, links, byline, status, gaps, and update trigger |

Interpretation:

- `0`: absent, false, or unusable;
- `1`: major problems;
- `2`: partially usable;
- `3`: solid with minor editing;
- `4`: review-ready and verifiable.

Target at least `80/100`, no critical failure, and no dimension below `3` before editorial approval.

## Anti-AI and anti-slop pass

Rewrite or remove:

- generic openings about the modern, fast-changing, or digital world;
- obvious rhetorical questions;
- formulaic lists of three adjectives;
- dramatic sentence fragments used as fake tension;
- bumper-sticker aphorisms standing in for explanation;
- repeated `not X, but Y` or `not only X, also Y` constructions;
- generic transitions and excessive signposting;
- a personal opening followed by an impersonal middle;
- headings such as `Background`, `Benefits`, `Results`, or `Conclusion` that reveal no information;
- conclusions that merely repeat the introduction;
- corporate fog, agency jargon, unexplained English terms, and unearned superlatives;
- identical article structures repeated across a series;
- padding added to hit a word count;
- metadata that promises more than the article delivers.

Do not ban a punctuation mark or word mechanically. Check frequency, function, voice, and whether a more precise construction exists.

## Truth and causality pass

For every important assertion, ask:

1. Is this fact, observation, estimate, inference, heuristic, or hypothesis?
2. What exactly does the source measure?
3. Is the evidence behavioral, self-reported, anecdotal, or normative?
4. Does the wording exceed the source’s population, period, channel, or result?
5. Is this the original source, a faithful secondary account, or source laundering through repeated summaries?
6. Are correlation, contribution, and causation distinguished?
7. What plausible alternative explanation remains?
8. What would make this conclusion false?
9. Does a legal, ethical, privacy, or permission gate apply?

## Final editorial package

- [ ] Canonical article and editorial notes are separated
- [ ] Claim ledger is current
- [ ] Sources are primary or justified
- [ ] Unknowns are visible
- [ ] Author and reviewer are real or explicitly pending
- [ ] Draft status is explicit
- [ ] Title and metadata describe the content honestly
- [ ] Internal links are relevant
- [ ] Alt text and accessible structure are planned
- [ ] Temporal claims have a review trigger
- [ ] No publish or deploy action has been taken implicitly
